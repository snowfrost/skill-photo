# Prompt Template

Use this template for a new composite. Replace bracketed values with facts visible in the source. Remove unused lines.

```text
Use case: identity-preserve style-transfer.
Asset type: one flat [landscape 4:3 | portrait 3:4] archival stamp composite.

Create a [LEFT-AND-RIGHT | TOP-AND-BOTTOM] DIRECT SPLICE.

[PHOTO PANEL] — approximately [55%] of the canvas:
Preserve the supplied photograph faithfully and realistically. The unique subject is [subject]. Keep [identity, pose, structure, viewpoint, perspective, relative positions, important text, objects, occlusions, environment, lighting, colors, and scene logic]. Crop only as needed to fit the panel. Do not add, remove, duplicate, reshape, recolor, or relocate anything.

For people: preserve exact identity, age, facial features, expression, hair, body proportions, hands, fingers, gestures, clothing, and relationships.

[PAPER PANEL] — approximately [45%] of the canvas:
Use clean warm off-white archival paper with fine visible fibers, subtle cream and pale-gray variation, and light scan residue. Keep it gently archival but not heavily yellowed, dirty, cracked, burned, stained, or distressed. Leave approximately 70% quiet blank paper.

Place one compact hand-pressed [CIRCULAR | SQUARE-FRAMED | PANORAMIC | ARCHED | SUBJECT-SILHOUETTE] seal in the [upper-left | upper-right | lower-left | lower-right] area. The entire seal-and-caption group occupies about 30% of the paper panel. Preserve [defining subject features].

Compress the source into a graphic print using rough screen-print, dry ink, worn halftone, uneven pressure, restrained ink loss, and slight registration error. Preserve the original color memory: [main hues]. Use [spot color] as the dominant localized ink with [supporting inks] kept muted. It must look pressed directly into the paper, never pasted as a miniature photograph.

[For square seals only: Add one complete unmistakably square ink border around the entire motif. Show all four sides. Use worn corners and slight broken ink, but keep the square boundary readable. No clean digital frame, photo mat, pasted card, or generic rectangle.]

DIRECT SPLICE RULE:
The two main rectangular panels meet edge-to-edge at one perfectly straight [vertical | horizontal] seam. No gradient, dissolve, feathering, overlap, ink bleed across the seam, page turn, gutter, book spread, diagonal split, mockup, or decorative separator.

Add small faded typewriter text near, never over, the seal. Spell exactly:
[TITLE]
[word / word / word]

Mood: quiet, restrained, graphic, tactile, and memory-like.

Negative constraints:
no changed source subject, no added or removed objects, no changed people, no fantasy scenery, no pasted-photo look, no glossy finish, no clean vector graphics, no decorative sticker, no scrapbook collage, no modern typography, no heavy paper aging, no dense text, no watermark, no page-turn effect.
```

## Targeted Revision Template

```text
Edit the supplied accepted composite. Change only [requested seal, border, scale, position, ink, caption, paper-age, or splice property].

Requested change: [precise instruction].

Keep invariant: the entire photographic panel, source crop, main seam, canvas ratio, paper texture, subject identity and count, scene geometry, original color relationships, negative-space ratio, caption spelling, and every unmentioned detail.

Do not regenerate the concept, change the source-photo region, introduce content, cross the seam, add a gradient, or create a page-turn effect.
```

## Validation Checklist

- One faithful photo panel and one paper panel only.
- Straight direct seam with no transition effect.
- Clean warm-white paper, not excessively old.
- Seal-and-caption group about 30%; blank paper about 70%.
- Seal sits in a corner and uses a subject-specific shape.
- Square seals show a complete readable four-sided ink border.
- Source subject, count, structure, text, people, and scene logic remain recognizable.
- Original hue memory remains visible through restrained ink.
- Caption spelling is exact and small.
- No invented content or watermark.
